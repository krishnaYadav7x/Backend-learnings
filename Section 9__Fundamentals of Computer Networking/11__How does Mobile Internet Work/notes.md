* **1. Mobile Tower**

  * The tower transmits and receives radio waves to communicate with our phone.
  * Our phone has an antenna that sends and receives these signals.

* **2. Connection to the Internet**

  * The tower connects to the mobile operator's network through fiber-optic cables or wireless links.
  * The operator's network connects our phone to the internet.

* **3. FTTT and FTTP**

  * **FTTT (Fiber to the Tower):** Fiber carries data to a mobile tower.
  * **FTTP (Fiber to the Premises):** Fiber reaches a home, office, or other premises.

* **4. Mobile Operator**

  * The operator's network manages SIM authentication, internet access, and mobile connectivity.
  * Its systems check our recharge plan and can restrict internet access if the plan expires.

* **5. IP Address**

  * Our phone's mobile data connection typically receives an IP address to communicate over the internet.
  * IPv4 and IPv6 are two versions of IP addresses.

* **6. How Data Travels**

  * **Phone → Radio waves → Tower → Operator network → Internet server.**
  * The server's response travels back through the same network path to our phone.

* **1. Mobile Tower**

  * A mobile tower provides wireless network coverage using radio waves.
  * A phone can connect to a nearby tower within its coverage area, which depends on factors such as frequency, terrain, and obstacles.
  * The tower does not work alone; it is connected to the mobile operator's network.

* **2. How Does a Phone Connect to a Tower?**

  * Our phone has antennas that transmit and receive radio signals.
  * The tower also has antennas that communicate with our phone.
  * When we turn on mobile data, our phone connects to the mobile network and can send and receive internet data.
  * The phone's modem helps convert digital data into radio signals and received radio signals back into digital data.

* **3. How Does the Tower Get Internet?**

  * The tower is connected to the mobile operator's network, often through fiber-optic cables or wireless links.
  * Fiber-optic cables carry data using light signals.
  * The tower forwards our data into the operator's network, which connects us to the internet.

* **4. FTTT and FTTP**

  * **FTTT (Fiber to the Tower):** Fiber-optic cable carries data to a mobile tower.
  * **FTTP (Fiber to the Premises):** Fiber-optic cable reaches a home, office, or other customer's premises.
  * In both cases, fiber helps carry data, but the final connection to a mobile phone is wireless.

* **5. Who Controls Our Internet Access?**

  * The mobile operator manages the network through its core network and billing systems, not simply through an office connected to the tower.
  * When we recharge our SIM, the operator's system records our plan and its validity.
  * When our plan expires, the operator can restrict mobile data access.
  * The tower provides the radio connection, while the operator's network manages authentication and access.

* **6. Why Does Our Phone Need an IP Address?**

  * An IP address helps identify a device or connection within an IP network so data can be routed to the correct destination.
  * When we use mobile data, the operator's network typically assigns or provides access to an IP address for our connection.
  * **IPv4** and **IPv6** are two versions of IP addresses.
  * When we turn on airplane mode, the mobile connection disconnects, so its IP address may disappear from our phone's settings.
  * When we turn mobile data back on, the phone reconnects and may receive an IP address again.
  * The operator checks our plan through its subscriber and billing systems, not simply by looking at our IP address.

* **7. Complete Journey of Mobile Internet**

  * Phone → Radio waves → Mobile tower → Operator's network → Internet server.
  * The server's response travels back through the network and tower to our phone.
