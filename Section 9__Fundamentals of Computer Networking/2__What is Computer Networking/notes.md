## What Is Networking?

- Networking is the process of connecting two or more devices so they can communicate, share data, and share resources with each other.

- **Communication:** Devices communicate with each other over a network.
- **Data Transfer:** Data is transferred between devices in the form of packets.
- **Resource Sharing:** Devices can share files, printers, and internet connections.
- **Internet Access:** Networking allows devices to communicate with servers worldwide.
- **Protocols:** Devices use rules such as TCP/IP and HTTP to communicate.

### Example: How Networking Works When You Open a Website

- You enter `google.com` in your browser.
- Your browser uses DNS to find the server's IP address.
- Your browser establishes a connection with the server.
- Your browser sends an HTTP request to the server.
- The server processes the request and sends an HTTP response.
- Your browser receives the response and displays the webpage.

### In Simple Words

- Networking allows devices to send and receive data from each other.
